01 · Strategy

The big idea

The problem

Smart locks are easy to open from an app, and just as easy to lose track of. Shared passwords, old phones, ex-tenants and "who unlocked the back door at 2 a.m.?" are everyday headaches for anyone managing more than one person and one door.

The promise

DM LockGuard puts every lock, person and phone behind one secure dashboard. Each person gets exactly the doors they need, on one registered phone, and every tap is written down.

The proof

Seven ready-made roles, one-device binding, step-up identity checks for unlocking, a tamper-evident audit log, live security alerts, and an onboarding tour (starring Pip the paperclip) that every new user must pass.

Three message pillars

Control

The right door, the right person, the right time.

Roles, time-limited access and schedules mean a dog-walker gets Tuesday mornings, not your whole life.

Confidence

"Locked" means the lock said so.

LockGuard never pretends. A door shows Locked only after the lock itself confirms it, and unknown states are shown honestly.

Accountability

Every tap, on the record.

Who, what, when, from which phone: the audit log is chained so tampering shows up.

Calls to action

  • Invite your first user
  • Accept Invitation

Honesty rule for all copy: only claim what the product does today. It is built for the Eufy Smart Lock C30 (model T85D0). Don't promise other lock brands, and don't invent customer quotes or numbers.
