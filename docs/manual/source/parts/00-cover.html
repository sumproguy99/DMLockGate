The five kinds of boxes

A Tech tip is a shortcut or a smarter way of doing something. Nice to know, never required. A Good to know box explains why something works the way it does. Something could go a bit wrong here: a step that is easy to miss, or a setting that surprises people. This one matters for safety or security. Doors, passwords, or your audit trail could be affected. Read it fully. Why do programmers prefer dark mode? Because light attracts bugs.
